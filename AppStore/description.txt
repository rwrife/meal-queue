Meal Queue is an offline weekly meal planner and shopping-list generator for home cooks who keep their own recipes and want the week's shopping done from what they actually own.

Keep your recipes, map out dinners and lunches for the week, and turn that plan into a single, merged shopping list scaled to the servings you actually eat. No accounts, no subscriptions, no cloud syncing, and no imported junk recipes you never asked for.

FEATURES

- Recipe library: store ingredients with quantities, prep steps, tags, photos, and base servings locally on your device.
- Week planner: lay out days with breakfast, lunch, dinner, or snack slots; adjust servings per meal; mark leftovers or eating out.
- Smart merged shopping list: combines like ingredients across recipes, handles unit math safely, and lets you add custom items and group them by aisle.
- Use-first pantry & freezer nudges: log loose pantry and freezer stock so you can use what you already have before buying more.
- Cook history: remember what was cooked when and how often, helping you rotate meals without thinking about it.
- Responsive design: platform-authentic experience on your phone at the store, your tablet in the kitchen, or your Windows PC at your desk.
- Privacy-first: all data stays local in SQLite storage on your device. Complete JSON backup and restore whenever you want. No ads, no tracking, and no network requests.
